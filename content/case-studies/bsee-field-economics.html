Illustrative Field Economics

Illustrative Example

This example describes the field economics generator, its saved metrics and saved cash flows. The production profile is synthetic and the financial inputs are assumed. No BSEE records are imported by this generator, and no client engagement or investment decision is represented.

Production and Financial Basis

The script constructs a 180-month profile with a 12-month ramp-up, a 24-month plateau at 100,000 barrels per day and exponential decline using an assumed annual rate of 8%. Monthly production uses an assumed 30.4 days per month.

Fixed financial assumptions are $75/bbl oil price, $18/bbl operating cost, $3.5 billion capital expenditure spread across the first 24 months, 18.75% royalty and a 10% annual discount rate. These are script inputs, not calibrated field data.

Limitations and Next Step

Monte Carlo simulation is not implemented. The generator evaluates one fixed set of assumptions; it does not import lease records or perform sensitivity sweeps. A validated IRR is not established, and a calibrated rebuild is pending.

Project use would require qualified production and cost inputs, a corrected return calculation and validation against the governing investment criteria. Analysis duration, consulting cost reductions and investment outcomes are not claimed.
