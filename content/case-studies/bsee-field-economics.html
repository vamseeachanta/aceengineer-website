Executive Summary

This field economics example illustrates a Gulf of Mexico deepwater production profile and financial model. Analysis is performed to calculate deterministic net present value (NPV), monthly cash flows and payback using synthetic production and assumed costs.

The saved illustrative output gives $6.1B NPV at a 10% discount rate and 32-month payback. The internal rate of return (IRR) is a hard-coded fallback placeholder, not a validated return. Monte Carlo simulation is not implemented in the linked script. The calibrated-data rebuild and IRR correction are pending under issue 106.

Data Transparency: The illustrative data generator constructs synthetic production rather than importing BSEE records. The saved metrics and cash flows reflect the script's assumed inputs and do not establish investment viability.

The Challenge

Investment Decision Timeline

Partners required comprehensive economics within 3 weeks to meet board meeting deadlines. Traditional consulting approaches would require 8-12 weeks.

Data Integration Complexity

BSEE production data required normalization and aggregation across multiple leases. Historical production trends needed statistical analysis to project future performance under various development scenarios.

Sensitivity Analysis Requirements

Investors demanded NPV analysis across oil price scenarios ($55-95/bbl), discount rates (8%-12%), and development cost variations (+/- 25%). Manual calculations would be error-prone and time-consuming.

Our Approach

1

Illustrative Production Data

A synthetic 15-year production profile is generated by the script. BSEE data collection, anomaly correction and regression against comparable fields are not performed by this generator.

2

Production Profile Development

The synthetic profile incorporates a 12-month ramp-up, a 24-month plateau at 100,000 barrels of oil per day, and exponential decline using an assumed 8% annual rate.

3

Financial Modeling and NPV/IRR Calculation

Monthly cash flows are calculated with assumed $3.5B capital expenditure, $18/bbl operating expenditure, 18.75% royalty and a 10% discount rate. Discounted cash flows are summed for NPV. The attempted numpy IRR calculation falls back to a hard-coded placeholder on error; a validated IRR is not established.

4

Deterministic Output

One fixed set of financial assumptions is evaluated. Cash flows are written to CSV and summary metrics to JSON. Sensitivity sweeps and probabilistic outcomes are not calculated by this script.

Technical Implementation

Data Processing Workflow

The linked generator uses pandas and numpy to construct the illustrative profile and cash flows:

  • Data Source: Synthetic production profile constructed in the script
  • Analysis Period: 180 months (15 years) from first oil
  • Production Aggregation: No lease-level aggregation is performed
  • Decline Modeling: Assumed exponential decline after ramp-up and plateau

Results

Illustrative Base Case

The saved illustrative output gives $6.1B NPV at a 10% discount rate and 32-month payback. Comparison against an investment hurdle rate is not established because the IRR remains a placeholder and the inputs are synthetic or assumed.

Price Sensitivity Not Established

The script evaluates a fixed oil price of $75/bbl. A price sweep and probabilistic downside assessment are not implemented.

Calibrated Analysis Pending

The calibrated-cost rebuild is tracked in issue 106. The illustrative generator does not establish a client investment decision or project approval.
