Offshore Platform Fatigue Assessment Methods

Method outline; case-specific outcomes not claimed

Method Scope

This overview outlines the inputs and checks needed for an offshore platform fatigue assessment. A completed client assessment, trained surrogate model, inspection comparison or improvement in accuracy is not claimed.

Assessment Inputs

  • Structural geometry, material properties and connection details
  • Environmental loading, operating history and monitoring data where available
  • Inspection records and the fatigue criteria applicable to the structure

Assessment Workflow

A project assessment would screen source data, recover stresses at the connections of interest, count stress cycles and calculate fatigue damage against the governing criteria. Monitoring and inspection records would need documented quality checks before comparison with model predictions.

A surrogate model would require retained training inputs, a defined range of use and independent prediction checks against the reference structural model. Those records are needed before a surrogate could be used to reconstruct operational stresses or support a fatigue-life conclusion.

Evidence Required for an Outcome

Case-specific training, validation, inspection results and performance comparisons are not claimed. No cost avoidance, joint reclassification, operating-life extension or regulatory approval is established by this method outline.

API RP 2A-WSD and DNV-RP-C203 are reference topics for fixed-platform structural and fatigue assessment. A standards reference does not establish compliance or acceptance of a project.
