Wind Turbine Foundation Analysis Methods

This page describes the method; it reports no project results.

Method Scope

This overview identifies the inputs and checks needed to analyse offshore wind foundations.

Analysis Inputs

  • Foundation geometry, material properties and geotechnical investigation data
  • Environmental conditions and turbine operating and transient load cases
  • Load histories and the design criteria applicable to the foundation

Assessment Workflow

A project assessment would combine load characterization, soil-structure interaction, stress recovery and fatigue damage calculations. Candidate geometries would be checked against the selected fatigue, strength and serviceability criteria.

A machine-learning surrogate model would require traceable training data and independent checks against reference simulations across the intended design range before its predictions could support a design decision.

Evidence Required for Design Use

Design use would require the source loads, model configuration, calculation outputs, validation cases and documented acceptance criteria.

DNV-ST-0126, DNVGL-RP-C203 and IEC 61400-3-1 are reference topics for support structures and fatigue assessment. Their mention does not establish compliance or certification of a design.
