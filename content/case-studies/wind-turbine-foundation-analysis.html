Wind Turbine Foundation Analysis Methods

Method outline; case-specific outcomes not claimed

Method Scope

This overview identifies the inputs and checks needed to analyse offshore wind foundations. Case-specific load histories, model training records, validation results, material savings and schedule improvements are not claimed.

Analysis Inputs

  • Foundation geometry, material properties and geotechnical investigation data
  • Environmental conditions and turbine operating and transient load cases
  • Load histories and the design criteria applicable to the foundation

Assessment Workflow

A project assessment would combine load characterization, soil-structure interaction, stress recovery and fatigue damage calculations. Candidate geometries would be checked against the selected fatigue, strength and serviceability criteria.

A surrogate model would require traceable training data and independent checks against reference simulations across the intended design range before its predictions could support a design decision. No trained ensemble, prediction error or simulation comparison is presented as an established result here.

Evidence Required for Design Use

Design use would require the source loads, model configuration, calculation outputs, validation cases and documented acceptance criteria. A project completion, quantified optimization or third-party certification is not claimed.

DNV-ST-0126, DNVGL-RP-C203 and IEC 61400-3-1 are reference topics for support structures and fatigue assessment. Their mention does not establish compliance or certification of a design.
