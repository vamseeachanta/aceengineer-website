Public data chart worked example

This example shows reported injury counts from the public BSEE calendar-year cards. It demonstrates how to read a chart and its source. It is not a GoM HSE analysis.

Inputs and method

The BSEE Offshore Incident Statistics Injuries cards give the inputs for calendar years 2023 and 2024. The values are transcribed without filtering, conversion or interpolation. Source date means reporting-period end; the page does not state a publication date for these cards. The retrieval time identifies the version read.

Answer and limits

The reported count increased by 20.0 injuries: 223 − 203 = 20.0. This difference describes counts. It does not measure a change in risk because no exposure denominator is supplied.

Acceptance: NOT_EVALUATED. The source supplies no acceptance limit. The example covers the Outer Continental Shelf, not a GoM-only subset. The dedicated HSE and drilling pages require the reviewed upstream exports.
