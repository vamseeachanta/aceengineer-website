How results are checked

Results are checked against an independent reference before they are signed. Three kinds of reference are used. This page shows a published example of each.

Three kinds of check

  • Analytical value: the result is compared with a closed-form or hand calculation for a case where one exists.
  • Second solver: the same model is run in a second program, and the responses are compared by frequency and heading.
  • Standard, edition and clause: each standards-derived constant or formula records the standard, edition and clause it comes from.

The engineer sets the acceptance criteria and reviews the governing cases. Each deliverable is signed by Vamsee Achanta, P.E., Principal Engineer.

Checked against an analytical value: tank sloshing CFD

  • The first sloshing mode of a partially filled rectangular tank is computed with OpenFOAM. The analytical first-mode frequency is 0.758054 Hz.
  • Seven free-decay cases vary the mesh and the timestep. The computed frequency differs from the analytical value by 0.2964 % to 0.3264 %.
  • The finest mesh, 25,600 cells, gives 0.755807 Hz.

Checked against a second solver: OrcaWave and AQWA diffraction

  • Wave diffraction and radiation for a floating hull are computed in OrcaWave and in AQWA.
  • The response amplitude operators are compared case by case, with matched units and phase conventions.
  • The comparison method is described in the diffraction reports. The numerical results are under source review.

Checked against a cited standard clause

  • Modules that carry citation records store the standard, edition and clause beside each standards-derived value.
  • A calculation stops with an error when its cited reference cannot be found.
  • 35 of 196 modules pass their cited checks (catalogue run 2026-10-17).
