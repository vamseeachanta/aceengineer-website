Vessel seakeeping — motions, natural periods, comfort

This page describes vessel seakeeping — motions, natural periods, comfort and the associated engineering assessment topics. Project use requires qualified inputs and engineering review.

Published capabilities

Assessment topics

  • Estimate vessel motions and accelerations from its RAOs and a sea state.
  • Report roll, pitch and heave natural periods.
  • Screen motion comfort and operability for the operation.

Questions

What does a seakeeping screen tell me?

How a vessel moves in a sea state — motions, accelerations and natural periods — and whether motion comfort and operability limits are met for the operation.

What inputs are needed?

The vessel's response amplitude operators (RAOs) and the sea state (wave height, period, spectrum).
