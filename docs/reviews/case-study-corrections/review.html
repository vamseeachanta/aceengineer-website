4 Basis

Each changed statement is checked against a public source or the inspected repository contents. Numerical acceptance is defined as agreement with the signed entries or recorded source counts; method acceptance is defined as correspondence with the implemented calculation.

A sample generator is not treated as evidence of measured coverage. A generic tutorial is not treated as evidence of a trained model for a particular structure.

8 Acronyms and Symbols

The terms used in the corrected statements are defined here. NPV means net present value; IRR means internal rate of return; FEA means finite element analysis; TSB means Transportation Safety Board of Canada; MAIB means Marine Accident Investigation Branch; BSEE means Bureau of Safety and Environmental Enforcement.

The suffix K denotes thousands and B denotes billions; dollar amounts are in US dollars. DNV identifies the named standards and certification organization; its name does not establish certification of these cases.
